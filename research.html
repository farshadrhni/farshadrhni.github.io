RESEARCH

Computational mechanics

Computational mechanics is the overarching field of my research. Within it, I develop and apply numerical methods—finite element methods (FEM) and isogeometric analysis (IGA)—to linear and nonlinear mechanical problems.

01 / Computational mechanics

Continuum & computational mechanics

Mechanical modeling of beam, plate, and shell structures, including linear and nonlinear behavior.

Continuum mechanics · Mechanical theories
02 / Numerical methods

Finite element & isogeometric methods

Formulation and implementation of finite element methods (FEM) and isogeometric analysis (IGA), including collocation.

FEM · IGA · Scientific programming
03 / Material modeling

Constitutive modeling & smart materials

Thermo-viscoelastic material modeling, smart materials, and shape-memory polymers.

Constitutive models · Thermo-viscoelasticity
04 / Research applications

Optimization & experimental work

Optimization, additive manufacturing, and thermo-mechanical characterization for computational research.

Optimization · 3D printing · DMA
