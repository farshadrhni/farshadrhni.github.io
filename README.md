## 👋 About Me

I am a researcher in **Computational Mechanics**, specializing in **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** and **Isogeometric Analysis (IGA)** for structural mechanics applications.  
My work focuses on advanced modeling, simulation, and optimization of beam, plate, and shell structures — including **functionally graded materials (FGMs)** and smart composites.

🏫 **Affiliations:**  
- University of Florence (Italy)  
- Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Spain — International Centre for Numerical Methods in Engineering (**CIMNE**)
