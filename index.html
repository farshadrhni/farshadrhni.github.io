Welcome to My Research Page

Farshad Rahmani is a researcher in computational mechanics specializing in advanced simulation and numerical methods, including Finite Element (FE) and Isogeometric Analysis (IGA). His research focuses on the modeling and optimization of beam, plate, and shell structures, with a particular interest in Functionally Graded Materials (FGMs) and smart composites.
