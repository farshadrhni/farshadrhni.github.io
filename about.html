Profile

Farshad Rahmani is a researcher and solver developer working in continuum, solid, and computational mechanics. He develops numerical formulations and computational solvers for beam, plate, and shell structures, using finite element methods and isogeometric analysis. His research interests include linear and nonlinear mechanics, constitutive modeling, smart materials, and optimization. His work combines scientific programming and numerical implementation with experimental characterization and additive manufacturing.
