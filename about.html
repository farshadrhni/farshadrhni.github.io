About me

Research in computational mechanics, spanning numerical formulations, material behavior, and engineering applications.

Research background

I am a researcher in computational mechanics working with finite element and isogeometric methods. My research includes the analysis and optimization of beams, plates, and shells, functionally graded materials, and smart composites.

My PhD research focuses on a computational framework for thermo-mechanically induced shape morphing of 4D-printed patient-specific shape-memory polymer stents. It connects geometrically exact beam formulations, isogeometric collocation, and thermo-viscoelastic material modeling.

Research interests

  • Isogeometric analysis and numerical methods
  • Nonlinear computational mechanics
  • Thermo-viscoelastic constitutive modeling
  • Shape-memory polymers and 4D printing
